<TLDR>
Points (PTS) are Quest's currency — earn them from tasks, daily check-ins, referrals, and milestones. Your total points set your rank (Unranked → Master) and your place on the monthly leaderboard, where top finishers win USDC.
</TLDR>

## Points (PTS)

You earn points from:

- **Completing tasks** and claiming campaign rewards
- **Daily check-ins** (+10 points, plus a growing streak)
- **Referral commissions** when people you invited earn
- **Rank milestones** — a one-time bonus the first time you reach a new rank

Points are credited instantly when you claim.

{/* ![Points and rank on your profile](/docs/quest/profile-overview.png) */}

## Ranks

Your **rank** is based purely on your total points. There are eight ranks:

| Rank | Points |
|------|--------|
| Unranked | 0 – 499 |
| Bronze | 500 – 1,499 |
| Silver | 1,500 – 3,499 |
| Gold | 3,500 – 7,499 |
| Platinum | 7,500 – 14,999 |
| Emerald | 15,000 – 29,999 |
| Diamond | 30,000 – 49,999 |
| Master | 50,000+ |

The first time you reach a new rank you can claim a one-time **tier reward** of bonus points.

## Seasons & the leaderboard

Each month is a **season** with its own leaderboard and a **USDC prize pool**. The leaderboard shows the top participants, your current position, and a countdown to the season reset.

{/* ![Season leaderboard with prize pool](/docs/quest/leaderboard.png) */}

At the end of the season the top ranks are paid out. Prize amounts are set per season — as an example, a season might pay:

| Rank | Reward (example) |
|------|------------------|
| 1st | 50 USDC + 5,000 pts |
| 2nd | 20 USDC + 3,000 pts |
| 3rd | 10 USDC + 2,000 pts |
| 4th – 10th | bonus points |

<Callout type="info">
Exact prize amounts are configured for each season and shown on the leaderboard — treat the numbers above as an example, not a guarantee. Add an email on your profile to get notified if you place.
</Callout>

## Daily check-in & streaks

Click the flame in the top nav once a day to claim **+10 points** and extend your **streak**. Streaks have their own leaderboard, so checking in every day pays off twice.
