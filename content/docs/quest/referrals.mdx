<TLDR>
Share your referral code or link. When people you invite earn points, you earn a commission — across three layers of referrals. Manage it all from the Referrals tab on your profile.
</TLDR>

## How referrals work

Every account has a **referral code** and a shareable **link**. When someone joins Quest through your link they become your referral — and you earn a **commission in points** whenever they earn.

Quest uses a **three-layer** program:

- **Layer 1** — people you invited directly
- **Layer 2** — people *they* invite
- **Layer 3** — one layer deeper again

Each layer earns you a commission at its own rate, so building an active network compounds over time.

## Getting your link

<Steps>
<Step>Open your **Profile** and go to the **Referrals** tab.</Step>
<Step>Copy your **referral code** or your full **share link**. You can also set a custom code.</Step>
<Step>Share it. New sign-ups through your link are added to your network, and you'll see them appear across the three layers.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Referral commissions are paid in points and credited automatically — there's nothing to claim. The more your network earns, the more you earn.
</Callout>
