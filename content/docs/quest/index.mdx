<TLDR>
Tasmil Quest is a rewards program: connect your wallet, join campaigns, complete tasks to earn points, and climb a monthly leaderboard for real USDC prizes. Live at [tasmil.finance/quest](https://tasmil.finance/quest).
</TLDR>

Quest turns exploring Tasmil and the wider Stellar ecosystem into a game. You complete **tasks** — follow on X, join a Discord, try a feature, make an on-chain action — to earn **points**. Points raise your **rank** and your place on a **monthly leaderboard**, where the top finishers split a real **USDC prize pool**.

{/* ![Quest Explore page](/docs/quest/explore-overview.png) */}

## The core loop

<Steps>
<Step>**Connect your wallet.** Click *Connect Wallet* in the top nav and sign the login request. Quest uses the same Stellar wallets as the main app (Freighter, xBull, Albedo).</Step>
<Step>**Join a campaign.** Browse campaigns on the Explore or Campaigns page, open one that interests you, and click *Join*.</Step>
<Step>**Complete tasks.** Each campaign is a checklist of tasks. Do the action, then **Verify** and **Claim** your points.</Step>
<Step>**Climb the leaderboard.** Points raise your rank and your position in the current season. At season end, the top ranks are paid out in USDC.</Step>
</Steps>

## Getting started

- You'll need a Stellar wallet (Freighter, xBull, or Albedo) — the same one you use for the main app.
- A small XLM balance covers network fees for any on-chain tasks.
- Open [tasmil.finance/quest](https://tasmil.finance/quest), connect, and join your first campaign.
