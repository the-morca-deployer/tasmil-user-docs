<TLDR>
Campaigns bundle tasks together. Complete a task, click Verify, then Claim your points. Social tasks (X, Discord, Telegram) require linking that account once. Some campaigns are daily and reset every 24 hours.
</TLDR>

## Campaigns

A **campaign** is a themed set of tasks — for example a partner launch or an ecosystem event. Each campaign shows its reward, participant count, start and end dates, and a progress bar tracking how many tasks you've finished.

{/* ![Campaign detail with task checklist](/docs/quest/campaign-detail.png) */}

- **Standard campaigns** run for a fixed period. Finish the tasks, then claim the campaign reward.
- **Daily campaigns** reset every 24 hours — complete the tasks, come back tomorrow, and do them again for more points.

Browse and filter campaigns (All / Ongoing / Closed) on the **Campaigns** page, or see featured ones on **Explore**.

## Task types

| Task | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **Follow / Retweet / Like / Comment on X** | Interact with a post or account on X (Twitter) |
| **Join Discord** | Join a Discord server |
| **Join Telegram** | Join a Telegram channel or group |
| **Visit Link** | Open a link and stay ~30 seconds so the visit is recorded |
| **On-chain action** | Interact with a specific contract (for example a swap or deposit) |
| **Daily check-in** | Claim your daily login points and keep your streak alive |

## Linking social accounts

Social tasks need to confirm the action really happened, so the first time you do an X, Discord, or Telegram task you'll **link that account**. The task button shows *Connect X / Discord / Telegram* until it's linked. You only link each account once — after that, tasks for that platform verify automatically.

{/* ![Linking a social account](/docs/quest/link-social.png) */}

## Verify → Claim

<Steps>
<Step>**Do the action.** Click the task button — it opens the target (an X post, a Discord invite, a link) in a new tab.</Step>
<Step>**Verify.** Come back and click *Verify Task*. Quest checks your linked account or the on-chain state.</Step>
<Step>**Claim.** Once verified, the button turns green — click *Claim* to credit the points to your balance.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Visit tasks open a short countdown page (about 30 seconds) to record the visit, then send you back to click Verify. Some tasks ask you to **sign a message** with your wallet instead — this is free and never moves funds.
</Callout>

After you finish every task in a standard campaign, click **Claim Reward** for the campaign bonus. Daily campaigns credit points per task instead, so there's no campaign-level claim.
