<TLDR>
Publishers launch automated DeFi strategies; users deposit into whichever one they like and earn hands-off yield through their own non-custodial keeper wallet. Live (on testnet) at [strategy.tasmil.finance](https://strategy.tasmil.finance).
</TLDR>

The Strategy Marketplace is a demo of a third-party strategy platform on Stellar/Soroban. Independent **publishers** launch automated yield strategies, and anyone can **deposit** into one to earn — without managing it themselves. Each strategy is an on-chain agent that trades a token pair on your behalf: watching a live price oracle to swap opportunistically, or dollar-cost-averaging on a schedule.

You always stay in control: deposits and withdrawals go through **your own keeper wallet** — a non-custodial smart wallet where the bot is only allowed to run the one strategy you activated.

## Getting test funds

Because it runs on testnet, you fund your wallet with free test XLM from the built-in **Faucet** (10,000 test XLM per wallet, from the Stellar friendbot). These tokens have no real value and exist only to try the demo.
