<TLDR>
Pick a strategy, connect your wallet, activate a non-custodial keeper wallet (two signatures), deposit XLM, and let the strategy run. Withdraw anytime — there's no lockup.
</TLDR>

## 1. Browse and pick a strategy

Open the **Marketplace** and filter by asset, risk (Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive), or sort by APY, TVL, or newest. Each card shows the strategy's APY, total value locked (TVL), fee, and number of users.

Open a strategy to see its details: current APY (a 7-day average), TVL, performance fee, how the strategy works, its APY history, and the list of active participants.

{/* ![Strategy detail page](/docs/strategy/strategy-detail.png) */}

## 2. Connect and activate your keeper wallet

<Steps>
<Step>**Connect your wallet.** Until you connect, the panel shows a prompt to view your position and deposit.</Step>
<Step>**Activate.** Setup takes **two signatures** — the first deploys your own **keeper wallet** (a smart wallet with the bot's session key built in); the second activates the strategy. Returning users switching strategies just re-configure and activate.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Your keeper wallet is **non-custodial** — you stay in control. The bot's session key is scoped to only the strategy you activated, so it can run that strategy but can't move your funds elsewhere. A keeper runs **one active strategy at a time**, so depositing into a different strategy re-runs the activation.
</Callout>

## 3. Deposit

Open the deposit panel for your active strategy, pick an asset (XLM or USDC — non-base assets are auto-swapped to the strategy's token), and enter an amount. You **sign one transaction to fund your keeper**, then the bot **automatically deposits it into the strategy**.

{/* ![Deposit panel](/docs/strategy/deposit.png) */}

## 4. Track and withdraw

Your position shows **Deposited / Value / P&L**, and the Dashboard → **Investing** tab lists everything you're in. To withdraw, use the same panel's **Withdraw** toggle — the bot pulls your funds from the strategy back to your wallet. There's **no lockup**.
