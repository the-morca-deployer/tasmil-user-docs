<TLDR>
Publishers launch an automated strategy with a four-step wizard, sign one transaction to deploy it, and it goes live immediately. You earn a performance fee on new profit and manage everything from the Publishing dashboard.
</TLDR>

## The publish wizard

Open **Publish** and fill in four steps:

<Steps>
<Step>**Identity.** Your publisher name, bio, website, and X handle. Your connected wallet address is your on-chain publisher identity.</Step>
<Step>**Details.** Name your strategy and choose its type (below), base asset (XLM on testnet), risk tier, performance fee, and tags.</Step>
<Step>**Review.** Check everything before deploying.</Step>
<Step>**Sign.** Sign one transaction to deploy and publish. The strategy goes **live and depositable right away**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Strategy types

| Type | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Auto-Swap · Soroswap** | Swaps into the target token when the DEX price beats the oracle's fair value |
| **Auto-Swap · Aquarius** | The same opportunistic swap, routed through the Aquarius AMM |
| **DCA · scheduled** | Dollar-cost-averages into the target on a fixed schedule |

You also set a **risk tier** (Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive), which controls the strategy's trade-size presets, and a **performance fee** — charged only on *new* profit above the high-water mark.

## Managing your strategies

From the Dashboard → **Publishing** tab you can:

- **Pause or resume** a strategy
- Monitor its **APY, TVL, and users**
- **Claim performance fees** you've earned (accrued on-chain), with a claim history

{/* ![Publishing dashboard](/docs/strategy/publishing-dashboard.png) */}

<Callout type="info">
Harvesting is automated by the platform — you don't harvest manually. As a strategy's value grows, your performance fee accrues and you claim it whenever you like.
</Callout>
